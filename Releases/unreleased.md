**Process refinement**

- 2026-11-15 **One review step for every round, optional size caps, and a stricter stop** (`Guides/Process/process-refinement-guide.md`, `Guides/Process/cold-review-prompt-template.md`; skai#90, #130, #135, #136): a change is first added to the target's design spec, then drafted as a proposal that rewrites the affected text; round 1 reviews the proposal and later rounds the applied text, each finding defects and shaping fixes to one standard, with compression part of every round rather than a separate review. A size cap is optional: proposed after round 1 triage from the settled size, recorded in the design spec, measured with `wc -c`, a proposed shape on a temporary copy, and met by tighter writing; a raise is proposed rather than content dropped, and dropping valid content or examples is a supervisor decision. When a fix becomes the next round's finding, the affected step or sentence is rewritten whole against the design spec. The reviewer reads the named files and everything they reference, tool code included; the refiner checks the guide's command and tool claims against the tool's current code or documentation without testing the tool. Only a fresh round over the current text that leaves nothing to apply, or a supervisor-approved ledger of the remaining findings, ends a pass. **Migration:** none; the design spec and findings log keep their paths and the findings log keeps its table.
