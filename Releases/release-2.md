# Release 2 — 2026-08-28

- 2026-08-13 **Cold-review template gains optional scoped/empirical-review slots.** Four new delete-if-inapplicable sections in `Guides/Process/cold-review-prompt-template.md`: `{{EXCLUDED_SURFACES}}` (files the reviewer must not read — blindness fence), `{{OBSERVED_FAILURES}}` (behavioral failure evidence, treated as part of the standard, with an answer-baking guard), `{{PROPOSED_EDITS}}` (draft edits to assess), and `{{REPORT_CHARTER}}` (scoped main report; out-of-charter findings go to an appendix). `Guides/Process/skill-refinement-guide.md` now notes findings may come from an empirical failure sweep via those slots. Purely additive — a standard full review fills the same slots as before and deletes the new sections; no paths or ids changed, nothing to re-run; hosts pick this up on a normal guide refresh.
- 2026-08-14 **Codification bar scoped to codification.** `Guides/Process/skill-refinement-guide.md`'s codification check (step 2) and recurrence threshold now state they gate rule-minting from incidental observations — not repairing deterministic document defects on first sight, nor sharpening an existing rule against a scoped review's chartered empirical failure. Content-only sharpening; no paths, ids, or re-runs.
- 2026-08-15 **Anti-over-engineering triage bar added.** `Guides/Process/skill-refinement-guide.md`: the codification check's log-only step now also covers strand-class findings — when the workflow has a designed escalation route, a gap that merely strands the operator is `log only` regardless of severity until it actually occurs; repair-at-any-likelihood is reserved for text that teaches a wrong action. `Guides/Process/cold-review-prompt-template.md` carries the reviewer-facing statement ("Stranded is not broken") so cold reviewers self-triage such findings. Derived from a 7-round refinement effort where ~3 of 29 findings codified paths the escalation net already caught. Content-only; no paths, ids, or re-runs.
- 2026-08-15 **Refinement supports declared size budgets.** `Guides/Process/skill-refinement-guide.md` gains one conditional paragraph (§ Repairing, "Size budgets"): when a skill's design spec declares a size budget for its guides, repairs state their net size cost, short headroom pairs additions with compensating trims (trims ride normal verification), a can't-fit fix routes to `human decision`, and closure records the guide set's net size change. Inert for skills without a declared budget. Content-only; no paths, ids, or re-runs.

**Migration:** none. All four entries are content-only — no assets added, moved, or renamed; no ids, managed blocks, or managed headers changed; nothing to re-run. Hosts pick these up on a normal guide refresh.
