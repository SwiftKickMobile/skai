added domains/suggestion.md

Why Supervisor input: the agreed suggestion information list.

This file defines individual collaborator suggestions, their notebook and collaborator associations, active status, reissue and replacement, and the meanings of their word counts and dates.

Identity

addedSUGGESTION-01

A suggestion represents one proposed edit by a collaborator for one notebook (NOTEBOOK-01). Proposed edits remain distinct even when they share the same collaborator and template (SUGGESTION-11). Suggestion information comes from the sync service, including its reported status when supplied (SUGGESTION-STATUS-01).

addedSUGGESTION-02

A collaborator may make more than one suggestion for the same notebook, including suggestions under different templates. Those suggestions remain distinct, and different templates may have active suggestions at the same time (SUGGESTION-03).

addedSUGGESTION-10

A suggestion's draft or final designation is the designation supplied with the suggestion. It is distinct from whether the suggestion is active or inactive (SUGGESTION-03).

Activity

addedSUGGESTION-03

A suggestion is active while it remains available for the owner to consider. Any of the following makes it inactive.

ConditionMeaning
ExpirationIts expiration date (SUGGESTION-08) has passed
SupersessionAnother suggestion has replaced it (SUGGESTION-12)
WithdrawalThe collaborator has withdrawn it
Reported outcomeIt is reported as Accepted, Rejected, Expired, Reverted or Merged (SUGGESTION-STATUS-03, SUGGESTION-STATUS-04, SUGGESTION-STATUS-05, SUGGESTION-STATUS-06, SUGGESTION-STATUS-07)

Word Counts And Dates

addedSUGGESTION-04

A suggestion carries these word counts, each with the meaning given here and no other.

CountMeaning
Current length estimateThe collaborator's estimate of the note's length in words; distinct from what the collaborator proposes (01)
Proposed lengthThe length the collaborator proposes under the named template (02)
Removed wordsWords the suggestion deletes from the note
Moved wordsWords the collaborator expects to relocate and discounts when estimating the owner's net change
Estimated net changeProposed length less removed words and moved words (03)
  • 01 The current length estimate is never presented as the proposed length.
  • 02 Where the proposed length covers only a first section, it means that first section, not the full length of a suggestion with possible later sections.
  • 03 Estimated net change does not imply that other edits or formatting changes have been counted. Where the proposed length covers a first section, the net relates to that first section and does not include possible later sections.

Reissue And Replacement

addedSUGGESTION-11

A revised or refreshed edit reissued by a collaborator is a new suggestion for the same notebook, with its own received date (SUGGESTION-08). The earlier edit remains a distinct suggestion. When the reissued edit replaces an earlier suggestion, that earlier suggestion is superseded and is reported as Expired (SUGGESTION-12, SUGGESTION-STATUS-05).

addedSUGGESTION-12

A superseded suggestion is an earlier edit that another suggestion has replaced, including a revised or refreshed edit (SUGGESTION-11). The replaced suggestion is reported as Expired (SUGGESTION-STATUS-05). Collaborator identity alone does not establish replacement: one collaborator can have distinct suggestions under different templates for the same notebook (SUGGESTION-02).

modified domains/workflow-stage.md

An owner's workflow is every note they are working on, from the first reminder through to a note that is archived or deleted. A stage is one step of that progression. This file covers what notebook stages, the stages are, what belongs in each, hybrid Inbox stage and what makes an entry in a stage schedule, and the conditions that call for the owner's attention.

Stages

What the stages are, what each holds, and which are terminal.

modifiedSTAGE-01

An owner's A workflow is divided into seven stage identifies the current phase or outcome of a notebook , from its first notes through to finished work or a deleted notebook . Publication proceeds through a reviewer's approval or by the owner sharing a link directly. The stages, in this order: Inbox, Drafting, Approved, Published, Locked, Archived, Deleted.

A notebook with a reminder in a downstream stage appears in both Inbox and its current stage; its appearance in the schedule does not change its current stage (STAGE-05).

STAGE-02

Inbox holds the notes the owner is due to revisit or has revisited without a decision. Drafting holds those awaiting further writing. Approved holds those a reviewer has signed off. Published holds those visible to readers. Locked holds those frozen for editing. Archived holds finished notes. Deleted holds notes that will not be kept.

Superseded by the stage table in STAGE-01.

Why Resolved decision: D1
modifiedSTAGE-03

Archived and Deleted are terminal: an entry that reaches either is finished with, and the owner is not expected to act on it again.

  • 01 A notebook in Archived or Deleted has no reminder available in the app.

Sync Service Ownership

The sync service decides the workflow; the app reports it.

unchangedSTAGE-12

An owner's workflow is the sync service's to determine. An owner cannot add an entry to a stage, move an entry between stages, or remove one.

unchangedSTAGE-13

A workflow entry the sync service stops sending is gone: unlike a reminder, a workflow entry has no retention period and leaves the owner's workflow as soon as it leaves the sync service's (REMINDER-08).

unchangedSTAGE-14

A workflow entry that cannot be understood is left out, and the rest of its stage is shown as usual. One unreadable entry must never cost the owner sight of the stage it belongs to.
