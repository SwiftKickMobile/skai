This file covers notebook stages, the hybrid Inbox stage and schedule, and the conditions that call for the owner's attention.

Stages

What the stages are, what each holds, and which are terminal.

unchangedSTAGE-01

A workflow stage identifies the current phase or outcome of a notebook, from its first notes through to finished work or a deleted notebook. Publication proceeds through a reviewer's approval or by the owner sharing a link directly. The stages, in this order:

StageHoldsTerminal
InboxNotebooks that have not yet left their first stage; also the owner's reminder schedule (01)No
DraftingNotebooks awaiting further writing after a reminder (02)No
ApprovedNotebooks whose reviewer has signed off the textNo
PublishedNotebooks visible to readers through a shared link rather than a personal invitationNo
LockedNotebooks shared through a link whose text is frozen for editingNo
ArchivedNotebooks whose work has finishedYes (STAGE-03)
DeletedNotebooks that will not be kept by the ownerYes (STAGE-03)
  • 01 A notebook starts in Inbox. This stage also serves as the owner's reminder schedule, including upcoming reminders for notebooks in downstream stages (REMINDER-26, LIST-02, LIST-03). A notebook with a reminder in a downstream stage appears in both Inbox and its current stage; its appearance in the schedule does not change its current stage (STAGE-05).
  • 02 A notebook that remains in Inbox after its reminder is completed transitions to Drafting on the next calendar day (REMINDER-05, STAGE-12).
unchangedSTAGE-02

Superseded by the stage table in STAGE-01.

unchangedSTAGE-03

Archived and Deleted are terminal: an entry that reaches either is finished with, and the owner is not expected to act on it again.

  • 01 A notebook in Archived or Deleted has no reminder available in the app.

Entries

What every entry in a stage carries, and how an entry relates to a reminder.

unchangedSTAGE-04

Every entry in a stage names a collaborator and a note title (PERSON-05), and carries the date the note reached that stage. That date is a calendar day with no time of day attached.

unchangedSTAGE-05

A notebook has one current workflow stage. When its stage changes, it leaves its previous stage. A reminder for that notebook can also appear in the Inbox schedule without changing the notebook's current stage (STAGE-01-01).

unchangedSTAGE-06

Every entry has an identifier that is stable while it remains in the workflow, so that the same note is recognizable from one viewing to the next.

unchangedSTAGE-07

Superseded by STAGE-16 and STAGE-03-01.

unchangedSTAGE-16

A reminder associated with a notebook describes the same collaborator and note (NOTEBOOK-02). A change in the notebook's stage does not change that association.

Attention

When an entry calls for the owner's attention, and how counts are reported.

unchangedSTAGE-08

A notebook in Drafting, Approved, Published or Locked with upcoming reminder information always calls for the owner's attention, whatever its stage date says (STAGE-16). Its appearance in the Inbox schedule follows STAGE-10 instead.

modifiedSTAGE-09

A notebook without upcoming reminder information calls for attention on terms set by its stage. stage, or by an expiring suggestion (01).

StageCalls for attention
DraftingAlways
ApprovedOnce more than ten days have passed since its stage date
PublishedOnce more than twenty-one days have passed since its stage date
LockedNever
ArchivedNever
DeletedNever
  • 01 A notebook with an active suggestion that expires within two days calls for attention whatever its stage, except in a terminal stage.
Why Design: the home view orders notebooks by urgency, and a suggestion expiring tomorrow is more urgent than a Drafting entry with no suggestions. The stage-only terms give that ordering no spec to stand on.
unchangedSTAGE-10

An entry in the Inbox stage calls for attention when the reminder it stands for is shown as overdue (REMINDER-19, LIST-09). A reminder that is snoozed, or whose completion is being synced, shows that instead and so does not count.

unchangedSTAGE-11

Each stage's entry count and attention count must be reportable without opening that stage.

  • 01 Inbox counts the upcoming reminders in its schedule; other stages count the notebooks they hold.
  • 02 A notebook represented in Inbox and a downstream stage contributes to both counts, and attention is determined separately under each stage's rules (STAGE-08, STAGE-09, STAGE-10, STAGE-01-01).

Sync Service Ownership

The sync service decides the workflow; the app reports it.

unchangedSTAGE-12

An owner's workflow is the sync service's to determine. An owner cannot add an entry to a stage, move an entry between stages, or remove one.

unchangedSTAGE-13

A workflow entry the sync service stops sending is gone: unlike a reminder, a workflow entry has no retention period and leaves the owner's workflow as soon as it leaves the sync service's (REMINDER-08).

unchangedSTAGE-14

A workflow entry that cannot be understood is left out, and the rest of its stage is shown as usual. One unreadable entry must never cost the owner sight of the stage it belongs to.
