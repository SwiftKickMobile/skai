# Glossary

Terms the product specs use, and the items that govern each.

### Terminal stage
Archived or Deleted: a stage the owner is not expected to act on again ([STAGE-03]).

> **Also called:** closed stage (support desk).

### Workflow entry
A collaborator and note in one stage of the owner's workflow, carrying the date it reached that stage ([STAGE-04]).
