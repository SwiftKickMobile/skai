Let a reader of the UI Map page see the map for one form factor, and see which scenes and routes exist only on some form factors, for the form-factor marking in skai#67.

The supervisor's input settled the designs; review them in the mockups on the ui-map-form-factors change page.

Design Changes

UI Map

addedV01add stateui map — form factors
  • 01 — A form-factor drop-down sits to the right of the domain filters, showing All by default. It appears only when the map declares two or more form factors. It's as wide as its longest choice, up to the width of the Changes toggle, and cuts off a longer choice.
  • 02 — A scene that exists on only some form factors shows the form-factor icon beside its other icons. A scene on every form factor shows none.
  • 03 — A route label shows the form-factor icon when a route in its box is used on only some form factors, after any note icon.
Why A reader can pick one form factor without losing the domain filters or search. Source: Supervisor input.
addedV02add stateui map — form factor menu

Opening the form-factor drop-down lists All, then each form factor the map declares, with the current one checked. The menu is as wide as its longest choice.

Why It works like the hops drop-down, which readers already know. Source: Supervisor input.
addedV03add stateui map — phone

With a form factor chosen, a scene that doesn't exist on it is grayed out the way search grays the scenes that don't match, and so is a route that isn't used there: its box and label, and a reused scene inside it. Connectors stay at full strength. A grayed-out reused scene loses its lighter look and grays like any other scene, keeping its reuse arrow. Grayed-out scenes don't respond to clicks. On a phone, the tablet-only route that puts the note beside the notes list and the tablet-only outline are grayed out.

Why A reader sees one form factor's map in place, without the layout shifting. Source: Supervisor input.
addedV17add stateui map — tablet

When the chosen form factor doesn't use the route a scene is drawn in full under, but uses another route into it, the two swap: the scene is drawn in full under the route that's used, and the other becomes a grayed reused scene. Nothing moves; the scene's own routes start from its full drawing. On a tablet, the note is drawn in full beside the notes list, and the phone push route with its reused note is grayed out.

Why A scene that exists on the chosen form factor reads as reached, by the route actually used there. Source: Supervisor input.
addedV04add stateui map — tablet scene selected
  • 01 — Graying out stays on while a scene is selected and the map shows only its connections: with Tablet chosen and the notes list selected, the phone-only push route and its reused note are grayed out, and the note is drawn in full beside the notes list.
  • 02 — In the side panel, a route that exists on only some form factors names them beside it. The panel lists every route whatever form factor is chosen.
Why The choice applies whatever else is selected, filtered or searched. Source: Supervisor input.
addedV05add stateui map — form factors scene selected

Selecting a scene that exists on only some form factors adds a Form factors section to the side panel, headed by the form-factor icon, with a chip for each form factor.

Why The side panel spells out what the scene's icon marks, as it does for notes and modal styles. Source: Supervisor input.
addedV14add stateui map — modal style scene selected

Selecting a scene with a modal style shows a Modal style section in the side panel, headed by the modal icon, with a chip naming the style. When the style differs by form factor, there's one chip per form factor, naming the form factor and its style.

Why The page already shows this section, but no design did; it's drawn here so the Form factors section can be checked against it. Source: Supervisor input.
addedV15add stateui map — modal style scene selected without form factors

On a map without form factors, the Modal style section has one chip naming the style, and the top bar has no form-factor drop-down.

Why Most maps have one form factor, so this is how the section usually looks. Source: Supervisor input.
addedV06add stateui map — form factors help

How to read this map gains a row for the form-factor icon after the modal style icon and one for a route label with it after the route note row, and its instructions say that choosing a form factor in the top bar grays out what doesn't exist on it.

Why The help explains every mark on a scene and every control in the top bar. Source: Supervisor input.

Components

modifiedV07modify componentIcon

Icon gains a Form Factor type: a tablet and a phone side by side.

Why A scene that exists on only some form factors needs a mark, and its side panel section a heading icon. Source: Supervisor input.
modifiedV08modify componentScene Cell

Scene Cell gains a Show Form Factor option that shows the form-factor icon after the modal style icon. It's off by default.

Why Any kind and state of scene can exist on only some form factors. Source: Supervisor input.
modifiedV09modify componentPanel Row

Panel Row gains an optional label before reused, naming the route's form factors in muted text. It's off by default.

Why The side panel shows which form factors a route is used on. Source: Supervisor input.
modifiedV10modify componentTop Bar

Top Bar gains a Show Form Factor option that puts the form-factor drop-down after the domain filters. It's off by default.

Why The production map's top bar holds the drop-down. Source: Supervisor input.
modifiedV11modify componentChange Top Bar

The Map tab of Change Top Bar gains the same Show Form Factor option, putting the drop-down between the domain filters and Changes.

Why A change package's map page filters by form factor too. Source: Supervisor input.
addedV12add componentForm Factor

The drop-down's button: the current choice and a chevron, in the hops drop-down's style. A choice too long for the width cap is cut off with an ellipsis.

Why The top bar needs a control for choosing a form factor. Source: Supervisor input.
addedV13add componentForm Factor Menu

The drop-down's open menu, in the hops menu's style: All, then each form factor, the current one checked.

Why The drop-down needs its list of choices. Source: Supervisor input.
modifiedV16modify componentRoute Label

Route Label gains a Show Form Factor option that shows the form-factor icon after the note icon. It's off by default.

Why A route label marks routes used on only some form factors. Source: Supervisor input.
